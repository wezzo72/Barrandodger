Statement of ethics

Truth should be free.

This archive is built so that primary documents, verification paths and research indexes remain publicly accessible without a paywall. Access to the record is not sold as a privilege.

Principles

  • Free access to the documentary record. Official PDFs hosted on public GitHub, indexes on this site, and the primary site at barrandodger.com are offered without charge for reading and verification.
  • No paywall on truth-seeking tools. Search, document lists, methodology and verification pages stay open.
  • Labels stay honest. Author’s Account, Allegation, Interpretation and Documented Fact are not collapsed into verdicts.
  • This site is not a court. Inclusion of a person, agency or allegation does not by itself establish wrongdoing.
  • Voluntary support is separate from access. Donations may support hosting, preservation and independent work. They do not unlock documents. They do not buy findings.

Support (optional)

If you wish to support free public access to this record, you may donate. Support is voluntary. The archive remains free either way.
