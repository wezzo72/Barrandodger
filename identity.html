Who is Barran Dodger?

Documented record

Public records and contemporaneous media identify Richard McLean as a Melbourne illustrator, musician and author of Recovered, Not Cured (Allen & Unwin, 2003). Later public profiles describe doctoral research at Victoria University and publications issued under the name Barran Dodger / Barran Resonance Dodger.

Author’s testimony

In later books, Medium essays and the public archives, McLean states that Barran Dodger is his public name for whistleblower testimony, sacred writing and documentary publication. Those statements are his account.

Interpretation
