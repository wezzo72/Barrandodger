About Dr Richard McLean

Public 2003 reporting identifies Richard McLean as a Melbourne illustrator and author who wrote Recovered, Not Cured about living with schizophrenia, and who worked as an illustrator connected with The Age.

Later public profiles describe further study at Victoria University, publications under Barran Dodger, and a large self-published documentary archive. Those later claims are sourced to the author’s sites, Medium, Apple Books and GitHub archives, and are labelled on each page.
