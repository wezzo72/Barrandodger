# 10 — This scan did not locate a judicial finding of the Rome Statute or state-terrorism characterisations

## Executive proposition

The archive's purpose, on its own homepage, is to make examination possible and to keep the difference between a document, a testimony, an allegation and a finding visible. The last fact this scan can state with the same confidence as the first is a negative: among the instruments opened, there is no judicial finding that crimes against humanity, state-enabled terrorism, or genocide by attrition occurred. The mandate is served by saying so. It would be violated by implying the opposite.

## Scope

Opened hosts and files listed in essays 01–09, plus the retrieved title and body language of barrandodger.com, plus the presentation homepage's own caution that published material contains serious allegations which should not be treated as independently established findings unless supported by competent authorities.

## Documentary findings

barrandodger.com, as retrieved, titles itself in part "Australian Government Corruption Exposed" and describes "a proven 35-year state-sanctioned campaign" including, in the site's words, police corruption, child sexual abuse stonewalling, genocide by attrition, and a documented attempted assassination. It states that the archive meets criteria for state-enabled terrorism, international political exile, and crimes against humanity under the Rome Statute, and that an ICC submission and an OHCHR reference UR/UST/23/AUS/17 are part of the public story. Those are claims on a project website.

The presentation homepage states the opposite methodological rule: allegations "should not be treated as independently established findings unless supported by competent authorities or other independently verified evidence." Theological writings are "the author's spiritual interpretation and testimony, not independently established factual or institutional findings."

The opened official instruments decide other questions: Comcare s 14 decline (essay 03); AAT covering letter affirming that decline (essay 03); Tredwell PID non-allocation (essay 04); APRA remit/PID refusal (essays 05–06); IBAC non-investigation (essay 06); AHRC statutory limits (essay 06). None of those instruments finds a Rome Statute crime, a terrorist act by the Australian state, or genocide.

Issue #2 forbids stating "X committed a crime" unless supported by an authoritative finding, and requires the sentence "No judicial finding has been identified in the material reviewed" where that is the fact.

Download counts ("1,100,000+" / "1,141,000+") and "zero defamation proceedings / zero factual rebuttals" appear on barrandodger.com. This scan did not independently count downloads and did not search every court registry for defamation proceedings. Those figures remain unverified here.

OHCHR reference UR/UST/23/AUS/17 and an ICC submission are repeatedly named. This scan did not retrieve an OHCHR or ICC public docket page confirming status, so the filing is treated as a project claim, not as a located international determination.

## Corroboration

The presentation homepage and issue #2 agree that the site is not a court and that allegations are not findings. The opened agency letters agree, by their silence on the point, that they are not Rome Statute judgments. Silence in a compensation letter is not a finding of innocence either. It is an absence of that characterisation in that letter.

## Contrary evidence

Absence of a located judicial finding is not proof that no court anywhere has ever used any of those words, and it is not proof that the author's allegations are false. It is proof that this scan, of the named public corpus, did not find that finding. The correct label is: not identified in the material reviewed.

Serious allegations remain on the gold tabs of the presentation site (Crimes, Who Did This to Me, Architecture of Annihilation). Those tabs are author's account and allegation unless a given page attaches an authoritative determination. This steel row does not adopt them.

## What the record establishes

- A — The presentation homepage and issue #2 require a distinction between allegation and finding, and describe the site as not a court.
- A — The opened Comcare, AAT, Federal Court PID, APRA, IBAC and AHRC instruments do not contain a judicial finding of Rome Statute crimes, state-enabled terrorism, or genocide.
- A — barrandodger.com uses those characterisations in its own voice.
- Unverified — download counts, "zero defamation," ICC/OHCHR docket status, as independent facts.

## What the record does not establish

It does not establish that the allegations are true. It does not establish that they are false. It does not establish that an ICC prosecutor has opened a case. It does not establish that "zero rebuttals" is a searched fact.

## Unresolved questions

Whether UR/UST/23/AUS/17 can be confirmed on an OHCHR public system; whether any Australian court has made findings on any subset of the allegations in proceedings not stored in the opened folders; whether defamation searches would in fact return zero.

## Evidence classification

No located judicial finding of the international-criminal characterisations: A, as a result of this scan of the named corpus. The characterisations themselves: C / E (author's allegation and interpretation) unless and until an authoritative finding is produced. Homepage caution: A, as a statement of method.
