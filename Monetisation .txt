OBJECTIVE

Transform the Barran Dodger websites from static archive/finding-aid websites into sustainable, professionally structured, monetisation-ready websites while preserving the integrity, independence and evidentiary character of the archive.

DO NOT turn the archive into a commercial-looking advertising site.

DO NOT allow advertising to obscure evidence, testimony, documents or navigation.

DO NOT make claims that visitors must donate, purchase something or click advertisements in order to access evidence.

The primary purpose remains preservation, documentation, research, testimony and public access.

The secondary purpose is to create legitimate revenue streams that can help fund:

	•	hosting
	•	domain costs
	•	archive preservation
	•	document digitisation
	•	research
	•	forensic analysis
	•	accessibility
	•	website development
	•	legal/documentary research
	•	publication
	•	ongoing archive maintenance
	•	the creator’s work

6. ADVERTISING

Prepare the websites for Google AdSense or another compliant advertising network.

Do not fabricate an AdSense publisher ID.

Create a central configuration variable such as:

ADSENSE_PUBLISHER_ID

and leave it unset until the actual approved publisher ID is supplied.

Prepare the site architecture so the actual AdSense code can subsequently be inserted into the HTML .

Use Google’s current verification/approval process.

Google requires the publisher to have access to the site’s HTML and reviews sites for compliance before ads can be shown.

Do not claim that AdSense approval has been obtained until it actually has.

7. AD PLACEMENT POLICY

Advertising must never interfere with:

	•	evidence
	•	PDF viewing
	•	legal documents
	•	testimony
	•	archive navigation
	•	accessibility
	•	emergency/help information
	•	important warnings
	•	document provenance

Preferred locations:

	•	restrained header/banner area
	•	sidebar on suitable desktop pages
	•	between major editorial sections
	•	end of long-form articles
	•	archive landing pages

Avoid:

	•	pop-ups covering evidence
	•	deceptive buttons
	•	fake download buttons
	•	advertisements disguised as archive links
	•	excessive advertising
	•	ads immediately beside sensitive allegations in a way that implies endorsement
	•	advertisements that make the archive difficult to read

Create an:

AD-FREE EVIDENCE VIEW

where practical.
