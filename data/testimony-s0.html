Taken together

Taken together, I say that I have endured a prolonged and cumulative pattern of abuse, neglect, surveillance, monitoring, harassment, intimidation, threats, physical assault, beating, injury, maiming, targeting, persecution, victimisation, discrimination, marginalisation, isolation, abandonment, ostracisation, exclusion, silencing, censorship, dismissal, disbelief, gaslighting, psychological pressure, psychological harm, trauma, manipulation, deception, misrepresentation, mischaracterisation, pathologisation, medicalisation, stigmatisation, stereotyping, blame, shame, humiliation, public humiliation, ridicule, mockery, vilification, demonisation, slander, defamation, reputational damage, public discrediting, framing, scapegoating, false portrayal, character assassination, moral judgement, sexual shaming, and the alleged weaponisation of my sexuality, faith, vulnerability, disability, mental-health history, testimony, disclosures and evidence; I say that I have been denied procedural fairness, meaningful participation, reasonable access to justice, legal assistance, Legal Aid, effective advocacy, meaningful complaint and investigation pathways, meaningful responses, recognition, protection, redress, compensation and accountability; subjected to institutional silence, indifference, bureaucratic obstruction, administrative delay, jurisdictional deflection, circularity, fragmentation, contradictory and inconsistent decision-making, exclusion from decisions and loss of autonomy, choice and control; subjected to financial deprivation, poverty, impoverishment, restricted access to resources and money, financial disadvantage, alleged exploitation, robbery, property loss, economic detriment, loss of employment, livelihood, accommodation and financial security; displaced, relocated and exiled from relationships, family, community and places of belonging; subjected to alleged medical mistreatment, neglect, involuntary or compulsory intervention, loss of bodily and personal autonomy and fear for my safety and life; and subjected to allegations of attempted murder, assassination, conspiracy, coordinated persecution, coordinated harassment, covert surveillance, technological or audio harassment and manipulation, deletion, alteration, suppression or destruction of evidence. I further say that the cumulative consequences have included physical, psychological, social, familial, spiritual, existential, legal, administrative, professional, reputational and financial harm; loss of dignity, reputation, trust, relationships, security, independence, livelihood, housing, career, community, voice, agency, equality, recognition and access to justice; prolonged uncertainty, distress, poverty, isolation, displacement and institutional conflict; secondary victimisation, institutional retraumatisation, cumulative and compounding harm; and what I describe as social death, total civil liquidation, administrative annihilation, institutional persecution, systemic victimisation, coordinated destruction and an attempt to erase me. These words constitute the fullest expression of my testimony and allegations: they are not a demand that anyone accept every conclusion without scrutiny, but an invitation to examine the underlying documents, chronology, correspondence, decisions, evidence and omissions and determine, independently and objectively, what the surviving record establishes, what it contradicts, what remains disputed, and what still requires investigation.
