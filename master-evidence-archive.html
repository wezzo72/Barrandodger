Archive purpose

This page is an additive finding aid for independent review. It organises material presented in the two specified public archives without treating allegations, testimony, analysis or submissions as established findings.

Impartial AI statement: This archive is a documentary finding aid. It is not a court judgment, government finding, medical finding, police finding or independent investigation. Inclusion of a person, institution, document or allegation does not establish wrongdoing, intent, conspiracy, liability or guilt. The archive records what its sources say and identifies what requires independent verification.

Verification and Review

  1. Verify authenticity and provenance of every document.
  2. Compare quotations against the original source.
  3. Check dates, chronology and document metadata.
  4. Identify missing context.
  5. Distinguish documented facts from allegation, testimony, interpretation and analysis.
  6. Identify contradictions and limiting evidence.
  7. Record plausible alternative explanations where relevant.
  8. Identify evidence needed to establish causation, loss, intent or liability.
  9. Record corrections transparently, retaining the prior record where practicable.

Claims requiring independent verification

  • Every underlying document represented by the public archive's numerical document counts.
  • Authenticity and provenance of declarations, affidavits, correspondence, medical records and legal filings.
  • Dates and procedural status of submissions to courts, oversight bodies, ICC and UNHCR.
  • Any alleged causal relationship between institutional decisions and financial, housing, employment or health losses.
  • Analytics and download figures displayed by the archive.
  • Any assertion of coordination, intent, surveillance, conspiracy, criminality or liability unless established by an authoritative finding.

Contradictions / gaps currently visible

  • The accessible archive displays different cumulative document/download figures in different sections. These should be reconciled against a dated underlying dataset.
  • The accessible landing page makes numerous strong analytical conclusions; those conclusions should not be treated as independent findings merely because they are labelled “AI”.
  • The inaccessible second archive and repository mean the present register cannot yet certify that every existing tab, link, declaration or chronology item has been captured.

Possible review, complaint, legal-aid or compensation pathways

Information for professional advice, not legal advice. Depending on the facts and jurisdiction, an independent reviewer may consider the relevant administrative review body, ombudsman, human-rights complaint mechanism, government complaint/review process, tribunal or court process, legal-aid service, community legal centre, whistleblower/public-interest disclosure mechanism, disability oversight body, or a professional compensation/insurance pathway. The correct pathway depends on the specific decision, date, jurisdiction, standing and limitation period and should be confirmed with an appropriately qualified adviser.
