This mirror is a static reconstruction of the public-facing reading experience around a preserved Barran Dodger website archive.

What is preserved

The catalogue uses the archive's manifest as its authoritative inventory. It exposes all 660 numbered records and links directly to available preserved HTML, PDF and link-list files inside barrandodger.com website backup/. The mirror does not duplicate those large files.

What is not changed

The repository root website, root index, archive directory, manifest, crawl script, assets, workflows and Pages configuration are outside /mirror/ and remain untouched. The mirror is separate from the current live website.

Known render exceptions

Eleven captured records contain little or no article body because the original site's rendering process failed. They remain discoverable and are labelled: “Captured page — original article body unavailable in the preserved rendering.” No missing text is reconstructed.

Inventory discrepancy

The two crop-circles records are retained as separate manifest records: /crop-circles-coded-glyphs-future and /crop-circles-coded-glyphs-disclosure. The mirror does not merge, rename or infer a correction.

Method

Lightweight metadata is loaded client-side from the preserved JSONL manifest. Search covers the complete inventory; source material is opened only when requested. Archived HTML is treated as historical content and is never executed as part of the mirror shell.
