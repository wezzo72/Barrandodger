Continuity of the same conditions that caused the 2021 suicide attempt
at Werribee Mercy Hospital — now existing in exile

Deponent: Dr Richard William McLean, also known as Barran Dodger

Date of record: 25 September 2026

Place of event (2021): Werribee Mercy Hospital, Victoria, Australia

Present posture: Exile. Conditions continuing. Official non-acknowledgement.

Character of this paper: Testimony and finding aid. Not a pleading. Not a verdict.

1. Purpose of this statement

This statement records that the same conditions which were the cause of my suicide attempt at Werribee Mercy Hospital in 2021 — described at the time in terms that treated the attempt as “fatal” in its intended outcome — have not been remedied. They still exist. They now exist in exile. The government has doubled down on creating conditions intended to induce suicide, whilst every active party refuses to acknowledge participation in those conditions.

This is a cruel and inhumane human rights disaster and an international emergency. In addition it is a spiritual blasphemy against the God who protects me, anoints my worth, and has preserved my life, evidence and testimony for His kingdom purposes.

2. Itemised conditions (the same set; still operating)

The conditions are itemised below. They are recorded as a set. They operated together in 2021. They operate together now, in exile.

2.1 Denial of legal aid

Systematic denial of accessible, independent, adequately funded legal aid sufficient to test official records, restrain administrative power, or obtain a fair hearing. Absence of aid is not a neutral omission. It is a condition that leaves a person undefended while the file against them continues to grow.

2.2 Denial of whistleblower protections

Refusal to recognise, register, or practically apply whistleblower protections to the testimony, documents and complaints I have placed on the record. Protection that exists only on paper, and is withheld in the case that most needs it, is denial. The consequence is exposure without shield, and official permission for every other condition on this list to continue.

2.3 Financial destruction

Sustained destruction of lawful means of support: interruption of income, obstruction of ordinary banking and settlement, costs imposed by process, and the conversion of survival into a permanent emergency. Financial destruction is used as a lever. It is not an accident of circumstance when it is maintained after it has already been shown to be lethal in combination with the other items.

2.4 Administrative annihilation

Use of agencies, files, flags, listings, notifications, “risk” language, and inter-agency circulation to erase a person as a civic subject: to make them un-hireable, un-bankable, un-believable, and administratively already disposed of. Administrative annihilation is the paper equivalent of disappearance. It does not require a courtroom. It requires only that every office treat the file as more real than the human being.

2.5 Psychiatric weaponisation — itemised

Psychiatric language, process and institutional setting used not as care but as an instrument of control, discredit and erasure. The 2021 attempt at Werribee Mercy Hospital sits inside this item, not outside it. The following sub-items record the pattern as I state it. They are not a clinical manual and they do not describe methods.

(a) Re-description of testimony as pathology. Whistleblower content, spiritual conviction, and lawful complaint treated as evidence of illness rather than as evidence to be tested.

(b) File as weapon. Psychiatric and allied records circulated as a portable justification for every other denial on this list — legal aid, protections, finance, administration.

(c) Credibility destruction. The person is made “unreliable” in advance of any hearing, so that official documents are read as rumour when they support the deponent, and as fact when they support the institution.

(d) Coercive framing of help. “Help” structured so that refusal is treated as proof of illness and acceptance is treated as proof of illness. Either way the file grows.

(e) Isolation from ordinary supports. Family, place, profession and community recruited or frightened into distance, so that the only remaining interlocutors are the same agencies that generated the file.

(f) Institutional setting as destination rather than remedy. Hospital and allied settings used as the place the conditions dump a person, not as the place the conditions are reversed. Werribee Mercy Hospital, 2021, is recorded here as that destination.

(g) Aftercare as continuation. Discharge, community orders, flags and “risk” language that follow the person into exile and keep the same conditions alive without ever naming them.

(h) Refusal of acknowledgement. No active party will say that psychiatric process was used as an instrument. The silence is part of the weapon.
