Formal objection and demand for action

I am writing to place my formal objection, personal testimony, documentary record and demands for accountability before NSW Trustee & Guardian.

Governing law and duties

My objection is that decisions affecting my accommodation, finances, vehicle, support arrangements and independence must be measured against the principles in section 39 of the NSW Trustee and Guardian Act 2009 (NSW), including paramount consideration of welfare and interests, minimal restriction of freedom, consideration of my views, encouragement of self-reliance, and protection from neglect, abuse and exploitation.

  • Administrative Decisions Review Act 1997 (NSW)
  • NSW Civil and Administrative Tribunal Act 2013 (NSW)
