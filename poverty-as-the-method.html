The sequence in other public deaths

Veronica Nelson, a Gunditjmara, Dja Dja Wurrung, Wiradjuri and Yorta Yorta woman, died at the Dame Phyllis Frost Centre on 2 January 2020. Coroner McGregor found the death preventable, and found the formal response of the Department of Justice and Community Safety, including the Justice Health report and the Justice Assurance and Review Office review, “grossly inadequate and misleading.” The Human Rights Law Centre summary is here. Victoria Legal Aid’s note of the findings is here. Care, in that file, was examined after the death and found wanting.

Naomi Williams, a Wiradjuri woman, 27 and pregnant, died at Tumut Hospital on 1 January 2016 from septicaemia. She had presented 18 times in the six months before. The last visit lasted 34 minutes. She was given paracetamol and sent home. The findings of Deputy State Coroner Grahame are NSWCorC 48 of 2019. The Guardian’s report of the inquest is here.

Errol Graham, 57, was found dead in a Nottingham flat in June 2018 by bailiffs who had come to evict him. He weighed 30 kilograms. His employment and support allowance had been stopped in 2017 after he did not attend an assessment. The coroner said the loss of income and the threat of eviction were likely the final stressors. The BBC report is here. In 2021 the High Court held that the department had acted lawfully. The family lost. The BBC report of that judgment is here. Lawful, in that case, included a death by starvation after the payment stopped.

Robodebt was described by the Royal Commission as a crude and cruel mechanism that made people feel like criminals, and a costly failure of public administration in human and economic terms. The government response of 13 November 2023 agreed or agreed in principle to all 56 recommendations. Minister Shorten’s published remarks paid respects “to those who took their own lives because of Robodebt and their families.” The respects were paid after the scheme, after the commission, and after the deaths. The media release is here.

What the sequence is

In each of these files the harm was administered as an ordinary decision: a benefit stopped, a discharge, a cell, a debt. The language of care, apology, or reform came after the body was found, or after a commission. The author’s sentence predicts that order in his own case. The public files show that the order has occurred. They do not prove that it has been ordered against him. A link is not a finding. The letters linked above are the letters.
