# Statement of civil erasure while still living

Dr Richard William McLean / Barran Dodger  
Drafted from the public GitHub corpora, 8 October 2026

I was erased from society while I was still breathing.

My identity, relevance, worth and value were treated as already withdrawn. I was excommunicated from the places where a person is counted — work, professional standing, housing that can be left, legal representation, media, kinship, and the ordinary assumption that a complaint will be read on its merits. I was decommissioned as a citizen who could still be believed. Survival was treated as improbable, and in some quarters as an administrative inconvenience. Everything that made me socially present was gone. My body remained.

That is the statement. What follows is the record I have put where a third party can open it.

## 2. What I mean by erasure

I do not mean that my death was registered. I mean civil and social death while the body continued.

I allege that, across more than three decades, a pattern of institutional and personal harm removed me from the systems that assign standing, and then used the resulting isolation as a reason not to hear me. In the personal statement hosted in the same repository — *Personal Statement of Exile, Institutional Detriment and the Search for Protection*, created 23 September 2026, eleven pages — I set out that pattern as allegation, not as a judicial finding. The heads I named there include:

- institutional abandonment and failure of protection
- financial restriction and loss of effective access to resources
- barriers to legal representation and access to justice
- forced dependency on accommodation and support arrangements from which I say I had no practical means of escape
- administrative processes that progressively reduced my ability to advocate for myself
- professional and social isolation
- characterisation of complaints as manifestations of mental illness rather than as matters requiring independent investigation
- what I report as electronic surveillance or unexplained audio harassment, including what I have described as V2K
- threats and safety concerns
- alleged mistreatment affecting my companion and therapy dog, Crystal
- what I allege to be coordinated reputational damage and social exclusion
- what I describe as political exile and displacement inside the country of my citizenship
- a cumulative pattern which I allege made independent legal, financial, medical, housing or media assistance extraordinarily difficult to obtain

The same statement says, and I repeat it here, that allegations are allegations unless independently established by a court, tribunal, official investigation or other authoritative finding. The existence of a document does not by itself establish that the alleged wrongdoing occurred.

## 4. How the erasure was carried, on my account

I allege that the removal was not one act. It was the interaction of systems that, taken separately, can each be given an administrative explanation.

**Published identity, then disbelief.** The 2003 book already placed childhood, psychiatry and disbelief on a public shelf. Later testimony, including the crimes catalogue and affidavits in the repository, alleges childhood sexual abuse, early targeting, psychiatric hospitalisation, forced medication, and threats under mental-health law. Status: author allegation. Compilation pointed to by the legal brief: `RETROSPECTIVE_STATEMENT_OF_TREATMENT.pdf`.

**Work and voice.** I account for employment in public systems, including public-hospital and DSS work, later used in the dispute over whether I had standing as a public official for public-interest disclosures. I allege unlawful termination by *The Age* and vilification by the *Herald Sun*. Status: author allegation, with the standing dispute traceable through the PIDs material and the government-documents release. Compensation pathways through WorkCover, Comcare, the AAT and the Federal Court are, on my account, acknowledgment without payment. Status: author allegation pending inspection of the specific orders in the zip and the `government-evidence` folder.

**Disability services as dependency.** As an NDIS participant I allege rent extraction, provider conflict — including Aligned Community Care, Upscale Care and Able Point — and an inability to sever the arrangement. I allege complaints to NDIS and DSS were not investigated on the merits. Status: author allegation, with the agency-adjacent pack at `coag-ndis-government-documentation.pdf`. The legal brief’s phrase for the housing period is internal exile: driven out of home, NDIS-tied housing, bankruptcy, and a subsistence figure I have stated as about $40 a week after rent. That figure is my account, recorded in the economic compilations, not an audited finding.

**Disclosures that did not receive a published merits answer.** Letters to the Prime Minister and the Attorney-General are hosted. They prove dispatch and date. They do not prove a decision on the substance. Public-interest disclosures are, on my account, rejected in part because I was said not to be a public official. If the institutions assessed the file, they can publish the assessment. If they did not, the closed door is the live issue.

**Law as a door that opened onto a different subject.** The inspectable Legal Aid NSW file identified in the brief is guardianship advice, not a published opinion on the human-rights, provider, disclosure and police-access complaints. I allege that access to a lawyer was the point at which erasure became difficult to reverse: without representation, the volume of the file could be left unassessed.

**Credibility used as the mechanism.** I allege that reports of surveillance, stalking, electronic interception and audio harassment — including what I call V2K — were then used to reframe me as delusional rather than as a complainant. Those reports are central to how I experienced the years. They are not laboratory-confirmed by the archive. Their documentary significance, as the legal brief states, is that they were reported into the same closed doors, and that the reporting itself was then available as a reason not to investigate. Whether that reframing was clinical judgment or institutional convenience is a question a merits process would have had to answer.

**The body left in place.** Homelessness, subsistence, the companion animal Crystal, and what I have called political exile inside my own citizenship are the condition in which the sentence was expected to finish itself. I remained. The archive is the refusal to let the remaining body be the only surviving record.

## 5. What I am not asking

I am not asking an independent reader to assume that every allegation is true.

I am not asking this statement to be read as a charge sheet, a police finding, or a judgment. The legal brief on the presentation site says plainly that it does not find murder, conspiracy, V2K as a proven technology, or any named person’s guilt. I adopt that limit.

I am asking that the allegations not be dismissed because they are extensive, difficult, or made by the person who says he lived them.

I am asking that the chronology be read as a whole. Individual decisions that look explainable in isolation can, together, remove autonomy, money, housing that can be left, representation, credibility, and the practical capacity to challenge the circumstances.

Faith-based writings in the prophetic-papers release, including the project’s spiritual reading of survival and vindication, are my interpretation. They are preserved separately from the documentary claims. They are not institutional findings.

## 6. The sentence I want kept with the file

I was still alive when the record of my relevance was closed.

Identity, worth and value were withdrawn in advance of any public merits determination I have been able to obtain. Survival was treated as improbable. The body was left. I have assembled the correspondence, the refusals, the compilations and the testimony into public repositories so that the erasure has to stand next to the paper, and so that a reader can say, item by item, what is established, what is alleged, what is disputed, and what has never been answered.

The archive is an invitation to test the record. It is not a demand that the reader accept my conclusions.
