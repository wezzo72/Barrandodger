DOCUMENT STATUS: Public forensic and documentary research report.

PURPOSE: To provide a comprehensive, independently reviewable framework through which the documentary record preserved by the Barran Dodger Archive may be examined, challenged, corroborated, contextualised and, where appropriate, referred to competent investigative, judicial, administrative, human-rights, academic or journalistic bodies.

IMPORTANT STATUS STATEMENT:
This report does not purport to constitute a judgment of a court, tribunal, royal commission, statutory inquiry or international court.
It does not transform an allegation into an established fact.
It does not assert that every interpretation contained within the archive has been independently proved.
It does not assert that similarities between the archive and the literature concerning “Targeted Individuals” or “gang-stalking” establish a common perpetrator, common program or government conspiracy.

Instead, this report preserves the distinction between:

  1. documentary fact;
  2. independently corroborated information;
  3. documented allegation or witness testimony;
  4. analytical inference;
  5. interpretation;
  6. unresolved evidentiary questions.

That distinction is fundamental to the integrity of the archive.
