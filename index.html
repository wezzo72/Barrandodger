About the Archive

Barran Dodger Archive — public documentary record

Through the Barran Dodger Archive and associated digital repositories, McLean has assembled government correspondence, legal and administrative records, research materials, personal testimony, creative works and other documentary material. The archive is designed not merely as autobiography, but as a public finding aid through which the underlying record can be examined.

The defining feature of the work is not simply what is written. It is what is preserved.

For decades, McLean has accumulated correspondence, government records, legal documents, institutional responses, personal records, research materials and other evidence relating to events that he says fundamentally altered the course of his life. Rather than allowing that material to remain dispersed across agencies, proceedings, correspondence chains and private files, he has sought to bring it together into a searchable and publicly accessible documentary archive.

Purpose

The Barran Dodger Archive is intended to provide readers with access to the underlying record.

Its purpose is not to require belief.

It is to make examination possible.

A testimony is testimony.

A document is a document.

An allegation is an allegation.

A finding requires evidence and proper determination.

Preserve the evidence. Preserve the testimony. Make the record public. Let the evidence be examined.

Australia — its agencies, services and platforms whose purpose is to protect vulnerable people like me, and its people, ganged up on me collectively, politically and systemically. It was and is a cowardly societal mobbing. I was physically exiled, isolated, impoverished and tortured.

In 2003 Allen & Unwin published my first autobiography, Recovered, Not Cured. I wrote the sex in that book publicly, in my own name, before any of these agencies had a file they could twist. The significance is that the testimony was already on a public shelf. It was not a secret they discovered. It was a record I had already made. After it was published, my former employer the Herald Sun weaponised words from the book. A weekend double-page spread carried the bold heading “MY DESCENT INTO MADNESS”. The Sunday Herald Sun spread is now in the archive. What followed was a barely veiled public character assassination. I was then fired from my job at The Age, where I was a news graphics artist and cartoonist. I was denied legal aid at the time, and there was no pathway to have it tested. The later Legal Aid restriction is the continuation, not the 2003 letter. Taken together, the book, the spread, the sacking and the refusal of a lawyer identified the public, private and professional way powerful people targeted me. It has lasted to the present day. The madness was not any real or perceived illness. It was the deceitful way my honest and courageous testimony was weaponised against me, systemically, politically and financially, from that point on. The targeting lasted decades. Its goal was my ruin, and the avoidance of accountability by those who participated in it.

For more than 35 years that detriment produced a documentary record. The Government’s own official documents, in its own words and on its own letterheads, are preserved here. Anyone can open them. The record includes a fatal suicide attempt, which the archive’s medical exhibit also calls a fatal injury. God revived me from that injury. The Victorian Ombudsman later rejected my complaint about it: Ben Calder, 5 October 2022.

Five years on, the conditions that solicited that attempt have continued: stonewalling, financial abuse, isolation, denial of Legal Aid, and no ordinary pathway to have the allegations tested. A public-interest disclosure was not allocated. APRA refused whistleblower status. The Australian Human Rights Commission wrote on 4 July 2023. McLean and Comcare was decided without the legal aid I had asked for.

The NDA, and the Federal Police wording I was given as “regretful” but “consensual”, is in the Ben NDIS text-message record. That PDF is the running record of those messages. In it, the other party says the only evidence he could give me was “a copy of the non disclosure agreement”, and that when he signed the agreement it disappeared from his phone with a notice that the device had been cleared of classified information. The same record preserves “consentual regretful sex”, “the police told me about the consensual regretted sex”, and “the agencies went back through phone records and believed she was paid to discredit you.” I publish that as the documentary record of what was said. It is not, by itself, an AFP finding.

That sentence — the agencies went back through phone records and believed she was paid to discredit you — is why I say I have never had equal treatment from police. A paid discrediting, once accepted, becomes the reason every later report is not investigated. The crimes that have occurred to me over 35 years, and the same list as a PDF, are the reading copy of that longer record. They are allegations and documentary pointers. They are not findings.

That same message record is where the alleged assassination is put in writing. My messages name Bill Shorten and a Lebanese criminal network sent “to erase me”, and say a minister may have ordered a hit by someone claiming to be a provider. The other party’s messages answer that account. It has not been disproven by an official finding, nor officially established as a fabrication, nor denied in any document I have been given. The published recording of the failed hit is the companion exhibit.

No one acknowledges the five-year marriage-like relationship I say I had with a former ASIO employee, Steve Iasonidis. I was hospitalised after I tried to obtain Legal Aid to have that relationship acknowledged for an equitable separation. Separately, I am restricted by the Commonwealth Ombudsman. I have found no restriction letter from the NACC in this repository, so I do not link that word.

I was betrayed, and I say that betrayal had a divine purpose: that I would rely on God, keep the evidence, and publish it. This website is that publication. It is not a court. The documents are here. I was not expected to survive the exile, the homelessness, the isolation, the character assassination, the administrative annihilation, or the intimate and family betrayal. I survived. The evidence survived with me.

The day of reckoning has arrived because the evidence has arrived.
