<p class="meta">
Barran Dodger Digital Documentary Archive · Master archive interface ·

<strong>Archive status:</strong>

This website is a documentary finding aid and presentation layer.
It is not a court, tribunal, police investigation, government inquiry,
medical assessment or independent forensic determination.

Documents, allegations, testimony and interpretations must not be
silently converted into established facts.

</div>

<h2>THE RECORD IS PUBLIC</h2>

<p>
I am not asking the reader to believe everything I say.
I am asking the reader to examine what I have documented.
</p>

<p>
If an allegation is wrong, identify the underlying document,
the contrary evidence and the reason the allegation is wrong.
If a disclosure was received, identify how it was assessed and
what response followed.
</p>

<p>
If legal assistance was refused, identify the reason, decision,
review mechanism and applicable criteria.
</p>

<p>
If an institution disputes an allegation, the archive should preserve
that response rather than suppressing it.
</p>

<p>
The purpose of this archive is therefore examination rather than belief.
</p>

<h2>IMPARTIAL AI STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE</h2>

<div class="method">

<p>
The archive should be read as a documentary corpus accompanied by
an analytical finding aid.
</p>

<p>
The existence of a document does not establish every proposition
contained within it.
</p>

<p>
The inclusion of an individual, organisation, agency or institution
does not establish guilt, conspiracy, criminality, intent, liability
or wrongdoing.
</p>

<p>
AI-generated analysis is an analytical aid. It is not a judicial,
governmental, police, medical or legal finding.
</p>

<p>
The archive is deliberately structured so that readers can distinguish
what a primary record establishes from what the author reports,
infers, interprets or alleges.
</p>

</div>

<h2>ACCOMMODATION, FINANCIAL DEPENDENCY AND CONTAINMENT</h2>

<p>
The author alleges that formal freedom to leave accommodation can be
different from practical freedom to leave where financial resources,
transport, alternative housing and support are unavailable.
</p>

<p>
The appropriate documentary test is therefore not simply whether a
person is technically permitted to leave. It is whether the available
financial, housing, support and administrative records demonstrate
a realistic ability to relocate.
</p>

<p>
This is an evidentiary question for the underlying records.
</p>

<h2>ELECTRONIC HARASSMENT AND WHAT I REPORT AS V2K</h2>

<div class="claim">

<span class="status H">H</span>

The author reports electronic interception, harassment, surveillance
and what he describes as V2K or voice-to-skull experiences.

These experiences are preserved as reported experiences and allegations.
The existence, mechanism or source of such phenomena is not established
by this archive merely because the report exists.

</div>

<p>
The archive therefore distinguishes:

</p>

<ul>
<li>what was experienced or perceived;</li>
<li>what was reported contemporaneously;</li>
<li>what evidence was supplied;</li>
<li>what mechanisms can independently be demonstrated;</li>
<li>alternative explanations requiring assessment;</li>
<li>and what remains unresolved.</li>
</ul>

<h2>NSW TRUSTEE AND GUARDIAN AND ACCESS TO JUSTICE</h2>

<p>
The author alleges that financial administration has materially
restricted his ability to obtain legal assistance.
</p>

<p>
This proposition should be assessed against financial statements,
decisions, correspondence, applications, legal-assistance requests,
reviews and the governing statutory framework.

<!-- METHODOLOGY -->

<section id="method" class="section">

<h1>METHODOLOGY</h1>

<h2>SOURCE HIERARCHY</h2>

<ol>

<li>
Judicial or authoritative determination.
</li>

<li>
Primary government record.
</li>

<li>
Contemporaneous correspondence or log.
</li>

<li>
Corroborated documentary material.
</li>

<li>
Author testimony, interpretation or speculation.
</li>

</ol>

<h2>NON-CONVERSION RULE</h2>

<div class="method">

Never silently convert:

<ul>
<li>allegation → fact</li>
<li>inference → fact</li>
<li>interpretation → fact</li>
<li>AI output → finding</li>
<li>hash → truth</li>
<li>receipt → corroboration</li>
<li>acknowledgment → agreement</li>
<li>silence → admission</li>
</ul>

</div>

<h2>CRYPTOGRAPHIC VERIFICATION</h2>

<p>
SHA-256 hashes, Git blob hashes, commit hashes, OpenTimestamps and
blockchain seals can assist in demonstrating file integrity,
provenance or existence at a particular time.
</p>

<p>
They do not independently establish the truth of the substantive
claims contained in a document.
</p>

<h2>DO NOT INVENT</h2>

<p>
The archive must not invent documents, people, events, quotations,
laws, court findings, government findings, statistics, financial
figures, corroboration, citations, sources, testimony, motives,
criminal conduct or causal relationships.
</p>

<h2>PROJECT-REPORTED COUNTS</h2>

<p>
The archive has historically used several different corpus counts.
Examples include figures for blockchain-authenticated documents,
government documents, PDFs, forensic analyses, blockchain seals and
total files.
</p>

<div class="notice">

Because the counts have varied between archive versions, numerical
claims should be labelled <strong>project-reported</strong> unless
reconciled against a dated machine-generated repository inventory.

</div>
