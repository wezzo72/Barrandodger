It is not a court judgment, independent forensic certification, or finding of liability.

I am Barran Resonance Dodger, also known as Dr Barran Dodger and Dr Richard William McLean. The Witness Remains is the continuing preservation of the documentary record of my life, professional and creative work, institutional encounters, personal testimony, spiritual writing and search for recognition, dignity and justice.

The archive brings together materials that I identify as government correspondence, administrative decisions, complaints, applications, personal statements, publications, literary and theological works, research notes, forensic analyses and other records. The purpose of preserving these materials is to make them accessible for examination, comparison and independent assessment.

My story is not limited to a single document or a single institutional encounter. It is also a record of the writing, reflection and interpretation through which I have attempted to understand my experiences. Documents can establish what was recorded, communicated or decided; testimony can explain how I experienced events; analysis can explore relationships between events; and spiritual writing can express the meaning I attach to my life. The archive should allow those different forms of material to be read together without treating them as interchangeable.

Three connected but distinct bodies of work

  • The documentary record — official correspondence, decisions, complaints, applications and other records that can be assessed according to provenance and evidentiary value.
  • The personal and analytical narrative — testimony, autobiographical writing and essays that interpret experiences and compare events across time.
  • The Gospel and prophetic corpus — spiritual and literary works that explore love, witness, survival, identity, the Joseph archetype, divine meaning and vindication.

The three dimensions can inform one another without being conflated. A Gospel can explain the spiritual meaning I attach to an experience. A personal statement can record my account of that experience. An official record can establish what an institution documented or decided. An analytical essay can propose a relationship between multiple records. Each should be identified by what it is.

Evidence classification (editorial framework)

Each significant proposition should be classified as one of:

  • Official record · Documented fact · Corroborated claim
  • Personal testimony · Documented allegation
  • Inference · Interpretation · Spiritual belief · Unresolved

Indicative A–E scale: A Established fact · B Corroborated claim · C Documented allegation · D Inference · E Speculation or unresolved interpretation. This is an editorial framework, not an external certification.
