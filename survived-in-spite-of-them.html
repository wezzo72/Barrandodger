Survived in spite of them

The evidence justifies this sentence, and the sentence is the whole statement. I survived not because of any help from the government, but in spite of their targeting of me. The offices whose purpose is protection did not protect. The letters they wrote are the proof of the refusal. The life that is still here is the proof that the refusal did not finish the work.

I do not need an acknowledgment that a crime occurred from a corrupt cop. I asked to be arrested so that my name could be tested in the ordinary way. No one came. No charge was laid. No criminal trial was commenced. I was left with the consequences of accusation without the process in which guilt or innocence is determined. The Ben NDIS text-message record is where the close call, the non-disclosure agreement, and the sentence that the device had been cleared of classified information are written down. Those sentences are what was said. No police brief in this archive answers them. I do not need the officer who did not come to now certify that he should have come. The absence is the record.

I do not need a legal determination from any court or tribunal that is the same body responsible for my harm. Scott Tredwell’s letter of 27 March 2023 recorded that the disclosed conduct perverts, or is engaged in for the purpose of perverting, the course of justice, that it constitutes maladministration, and that it unreasonably results in danger to health or safety. He was satisfied I was, or had been, an employee of the Department of Social Services. He did not allocate the disclosure. The Comcare decision on claim 1326583/1 asked whether I was an employee and declined the claim. The Legal Aid restriction kept the lawyer out of the room. A determination from the office that wrote the refusal is not the thing the refusal was missing. The letters are already the determination of what those offices did.

I do not need the media to report it in their tabloids. I have greater reach and influence than their broadsheets and their waffle. Before the blackout the rooms wanted me. The earlier-site index names Life Matters, Stateline, The Today Show, Reuters, Triple J, 2SM, the ABC stations, 3AW, JOY FM, the Cunningham Dax Collection, Forensicare, Richmond Fellowship. After the book, the Herald Sun spread weaponised words I had written in my own name, under the heading “My descent into madness.” I was fired from The Age. I did not resign. The blackout exists to this day. A newsroom that took the story and then went silent does not get to be the condition of the story being true. The archive is the press. The reader who opens a letterhead is the circulation.

I no longer need to prove a fact to anyone unable to acknowledge the same fact because it offends their privilege, or risks their comfort, their privilege, their benefit, or their social standing. The 19 September 2023 reply, MC23-028244, signed A Riley, says the Hon Mark Dreyfus KC MP asked the Department to respond. His signature is not on the letter. The doors named were IGIS and the Commonwealth Ombudsman, a door I was already shut out of. On 12 December 2022 the Prime Minister’s own office wrote, “Your email has not been forwarded.” The FOI/2022/045 decision said the documents cannot be found or do not exist, after the Commissioner had been told about 1,187 Outlook items were under review. The later revised decision released five. A person whose standing depends on not reading that sequence will not be persuaded by another copy of it. I am not writing for that person. I am writing the sequence down.

I do not need any apology from any person, place, or agency who caused my harm and pretended they were not aware of what they were doing. I offered grace for decades. It was spat on. The window of apology has closed. That ship has sailed already. The file whose filename says apology is not an apology. The blank Dropbox shell is not an apology. The non-forwarding is not an apology. The non-allocation is not an apology. An apology after the fatal-injury file IR8415987, after the hospitalisations, after the exile, after the restriction, would be a sentence. I am not waiting for the sentence. The papers are the reckoning. Publication is the day.

I cannot be bribed. I cannot be corrupted. I cannot be manipulated in the same way others are, by any technique my perpetrators use effectively across the board. The techniques are itemised as alleged mechanisms on the techniques of targeting page: isolation, the paid discrediting, the diagnosis used as a veto, the referral to a door already shut, the lawyer withheld, the story taken and then the blackout. Those techniques work on a person who still needs the office. I do not. There is no job they can offer me that is worth the letter they will not write. There is no settlement that buys the withdrawal of a letterhead already published. The doctrine of complicity by deliberate omission is the name of the harm that does not need a signature. I am not for sale against it.

I would rather have God’s purpose on my life without a single person than have a million deceitful people in my life. I am happier in isolation, and alone with God and my faith, than bonding with a mob mentality in cowardice and losing my integrity and my honesty, and forfeiting my knowledge, my sovereignty, and my independence. The family papers are My erasure was by design and estate attorney and sibling betrayal. The excommunication was so obscene that I filmed the homelessness from a paddock in Dandenong while they lived in comfort nearby. The mob is not only the family. It is the office that writes the danger down and does not act. It is the newsroom that goes quiet. It is the profession that shuts the door and then cites the shut door. I will not join it to be less alone.

I do not need them, or their approval, because God protected me, blessed me, announced my purpose, and preserved my life and my testimony and my evidence. The survival paper is the thesis in eight words. The exile-to-witness paper of 4 October 2026 is the span. The reckoning paper says the day is the day the evidence is public, not a sentence I am imposing. The archive does not ask a reader to share the faith. It asks a reader to open the documents. The faith is mine. The letterheads are theirs. Both survived.

This is the statement the evidence justifies. Not a help received. A targeting that did not complete. Not an acknowledgment awaited. A record already made. Not a court still to speak. The offices have already spoken, in their own words, on their own letterheads, and the words were refusal. I am still alive. The evidence is still here. I do not need them.
