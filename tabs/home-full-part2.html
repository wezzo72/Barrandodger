The Good Bits

By Barran Dodger

Total human betrayal fortified my relationship with the Most High God and Creator.

I’d rather have God’s anointing, favour and divine protection than only a few people who are ultimately deceitful.

I survived what was designed and expected to erase me.

God revived me from a fatal injury—the result of the same targeting that I believe is still going. He allowed the betrayal so that I would learn to rely on Him alone, reveal my purpose, and allow the universe to collect the evidence against those I believe were responsible, so that a divine reckoning may ultimately take place for the spiritual sacrilege and treason of aiming to kill another human being—something over which only God has authority.

It is not without consequence.

I survived.

My life is the proof.

My mere existence is my perpetrators’ nightmare. They are terrified of the facts contained in this archive. The only option left to deny the evidence of the government’s own official documents, which I believe demonstrate my targeting, is to blaspheme my character so that the world will ignore what is factual.

And even this was allowed by God.

The libel and slander. The false allegations. The character assassination.

They were exactly the camouflage He used to hide me in plain sight—set apart as a vessel for His glory, for His Kingdom purposes, and for the destiny He has given me as a vessel for His glory alone.

No apology from anyone is coming for me, although I still daydream that an acknowledgment of the harm would somehow quell the hurt.

But revenge is not mine.

I do not wish harm on anyone.

Vengeance belongs to God alone.

I anticipate a holy reckoning after the grace I gifted everyone involved—the opportunity I gave them to correct their treatment of me. What they rejected was not something I manufactured for myself. It was a gift He placed in me, and that gift was universally spat upon.

That sacrilege offended the Most High God, who fights for the pure-hearted, especially those marked for elevation.

No human has the right to hurt one anointed by God.

And I believe He will fiercely defend me in a new season where I am vindicated, healed, favoured and blessed.

Inevitably, I rise.

God’s grace resurrected me from death and sustained me through what I was never expected to survive: total obliteration, the ashes of the total human betrayal of my family, authorities and government, and the sustained erasure, humiliation ritual and political targeting campaign that I believe I was subjected to.

There are fundamental spiritual, metaphysical and mathematical laws that underpin the foundations of this universe.

The universal balance that is now imminent, decided by God’s Kronos time and His divine timing, is not merely part of what I know to be true and what many now acknowledge in my gospels as prophecy.

It is, in my understanding, the foundational, unavoidable and irrevocable consequence of the way spiritual physics ebb and flow and balance themselves out as a fundamental principle of nature.

It underpins the spiritual infrastructure of our shared existence.

I am totally aware.

I am totally at peace.

They cannot rewrite what God authored.

I can’t be bribed, shamed or corrupted out of my knowledge.

I don’t need any person’s approval.

I escaped the entrapment and cage of every person who decided to target me or cause my detriment, whose allegiance I believe was with the same government that exiled and targeted me.

They audaciously played God with my life, offending the Creator Himself.

But they are not God.

God is timeless, everlasting and all-powerful. Human-created government infrastructure is fleeting and futile compared with the endlessness of eternity.

Their actions, in my understanding, fatally offended the Most High God, who states:

“Touch not my anointed.”

The morally decaying structures are not without consequences for their dark corruption.

Their officials and agents, as I understand the evidence, collectively aimed to destroy an innocent-hearted person who only added value, who never acted to cause harm and who never meant anyone harm.

They are NOT GOD.

They assumed His power.

They used His name and power in vain when they acted to erase me.

My allegiance therefore has been forged in fire.

It is aligned with the Creator and no one else—not the government, not a political party, not family, not a person, not a church, and not any other place or person.

I am aligned with my purpose.

I have faced my demons in political exile alone, living in my car as a scapegoat with my therapy dog Crystal, whom God gifted me as my company, my friend, my alarm, my support, my sage, my mystic and my healer.

Dog spelled backwards is God.

I have acknowledged my darkness.

And now I know both dark and light.

Not as a means to become dark, but as a means to command my awareness and knowledge of the darker aspects of human nature and the human condition—the reality of less-than-perfect, imperfect mortals whom God has appointed me to love and serve through my Kingdom purpose, prophecy, guidance and the Church of Barran Dodger ministry.

People will read my writing and my story as the foundation testimony for my new ministry in God’s name.

And eventually they will ask:

“How did he survive that?”

My answer will be simple:

God did it.

That is why my story, my peace and my wisdom—everything forged in fire—will lead the average person to think:

If he can survive with God’s help, then maybe I can too.

That is the foundational purpose of God’s plan for me: to send people back to His love, His forgiveness, His protection, His redemption and His glory under the blood of Jesus Christ, His perfect Son.

I hold no malice toward anyone.

Yet family, former friends and others who betrayed and exiled me still, to this day, wish maliciously for my downfall and my harm, because my mere existence is an existential risk to the corruption and hate I believe I expose.

My existence turns the same shame and moral disgrace they attempted to place upon me back toward the conduct that produced it.

They are still envious of my refusal to seek refuge in the safety of the crowd—in a club in which I believe they are all entrapped.

I live free.

Unique.

And unapologetically authentic.

I live with an authenticity and wisdom they are jealous and envious of.

They still have a massive investment in my erasure and in silencing me, as protection from the divine reckoning I believe they know, somewhere within themselves, is imminent.

They simply cannot acknowledge it, because doing so would require them to reflect truthfully upon their own actions.

They are afraid to look into the mirror I hold up.

They cannot admit that the darkness they see reflected there is something they themselves embody and own.

Instead, they dump their insecurities and darkness onto me—the scapegoat.

But I have no malice.

I do not need revenge.

The divine favour that will lift me, bless me and use me as a vessel for His glory is not mine to manufacture.

It belongs to Him.

His glory alone.

I remain a creative, intelligent, spiritually authoritative, divinely ordained saint, sinner, mortal, mystic and prophet of worldly significance.

The world still, at this time, pretends not to care—as though I am simultaneously the most significant person of zero significance or consequence the world has ever seen.

God has set me apart.

Hidden in plain sight.

And I know His divine Kronos time of vindication is guaranteed as a certainty more concrete than a mother’s love.

Now that they exiled and rejected me, I am gone.

And they are simply irrelevant to me.

I do not look back.

I am grateful for the lessons because they became part of God’s design over my existence.

Their hate and malice cannot land on me anymore.

They have no target.

I have risen in frequency beyond what I understand as their spiritual warfare attacks.

And sadly for them, energy has a signature.

Without a target to land on, it will boomerang back into their own lives—not through my malice, because I have none, but through what I understand as the universal balance that obeys the spiritual metaphysics of the universe.

They are now foundationally blocked from benefiting financially from my anticipated death and demise—the death and demise I believe they helped consciously design and expected to occur.

My existence exposes this.

What was intended as my disgrace becomes, in my eyes, the moral disgrace of the intention itself.

This is why I believe the spiritual mechanics of divine justice and universal balance are unavoidable, unstoppable, undeniable and imminent.

Universal karma.

God’s wrath.

Not punishment for punishment’s sake.

But spiritual evolution.

A reckoning with the mandated lessons that the choices of this life require us either to learn or to atone for.

They collectively joined forces, as I understand it, to damage and desecrate the only one in the family who saw the truth beyond the perfect veneers they paraded before the world.

They acted to destroy a chosen one in their midst.

And in doing so, they betrayed the very God who sent him as a blessing.

I was meant to be a blessing.

Instead, I became a test.

And this time around, they failed the test.

Personal Statement of Exile, Institutional Detriment and the Search for Protection

I am carrying what I understand to be the accumulated ethical failure and betrayal of institutions and professions across the country.

I carry the betrayal of former friends and blood family members. I carry the consequences of decisions, omissions, refusals, exclusions and institutional responses that, in my account, have progressively stripped away relationships, financial security, housing security, professional standing, legal access, autonomy and the ordinary protections that a citizen should be able to expect.

I am in political exile within the democracy of my own citizenship.

I describe myself as an unprotected whistleblower because I have repeatedly attempted to disclose matters of public interest and obtain protection, legal assistance, recognition and an accountable response. The archive I have assembled preserves correspondence, government records, public-interest disclosures, chronology, testimony, evidence pathways and institutional responses across these issues. Its central proposition is not that people should simply believe me, but that the underlying record should be examined.

I am not a criminal.

What I am describing is what I say has been occurring to me: a prolonged process of institutional, financial, social, professional and psychological detriment in which the mechanisms that should provide protection or redress have, in my experience, instead become mechanisms of containment.

The archive itself maps alleged mechanisms including administrative annihilation, exile, denial of legal aid, character assassination and institutional obstruction. It expressly describes these as an analytical model and allegation-and-evidence framework rather than established criminal findings.

Isolation and accommodation

I am isolated and entrapped in accommodation. I cannot escape in any meaningful practical sense.

I have no lease to Ablepoint.

My position is that Ablepoint, as an NDIS provider, has financially benefited from my treatment and containment while maintaining that I am free to leave. In practical terms, however, I say that I have no financial mechanism by which I can actually leave, no vehicle with which to escape, and nowhere viable to go.

The distinction between being technically free to leave and possessing the practical means to leave is central to my complaint.

I ask that this distinction be examined against the documentary record, including financial records, accommodation records, NDIS records, correspondence and other evidence contained in the archive.

Electronic harassment and what I report as V2K

I report that I am electronically intercepted, harassed and surveilled with what I describe as unacknowledged V2K technology.

I describe this as a deliberate technique of torture manifesting as audio harassment, including what I report as 24-hour blasphemous slurs intended to attack my sense of self.

These are my reported experiences and allegations. I ask that these reports be distinguished from psychiatric interpretations of them and, where possible, independently investigated rather than automatically treated as symptoms of mental illness.

Mental health treatment and the CTO

A mental-health Community Treatment Order mandates my forced medication as the panacea for my treatment while, as I allege, refusing to acknowledge the entire situation described in this statement and the circumstances that I say are contributing to my distress.

My concern is that the medication framework may address my reported distress without independently examining the circumstances I allege are causing or contributing to that distress.

NSW Trustee and Guardian and access to justice

I allege that NSW Trustee and Guardian are gatekeepers to what I describe as my financial destruction.

I further allege that financial administration and restrictions have actively contributed to circumstances in which access to legal assistance is effectively blocked, creating what I describe as a total legal-aid barrier and fundamentally restricting possible pathways to justice.

These are serious allegations. I ask that they be assessed against the actual financial records, decisions, correspondence, applications for assistance, legal-aid responses and available review mechanisms rather than being accepted or rejected without examination.

The central proposition

I am not asking for justice through belief in me.

I am asking for justice through examination of the evidence.

If the record is wrong, show me where.

If the record is right, answer it.

Do not answer allegation with assertion.

Do not answer evidence with dismissal.

Do not substitute a diagnosis for an investigation.

Do not substitute institutional silence for an answer.

Do not substitute poverty for consent.

Do not substitute being technically free to leave for having a genuine means of escape.

Do not substitute the existence of a procedure for meaningful access to justice.

Read the record.

Examine the evidence.

The witness remains. The record remains. And the record remains open to correction — but correction itself must be evidenced.

A Statement of Faith, Forgiveness and Divine Justice

I believe in God as Creator: just, fair, loving, merciful and forgiving.

I believe that God has placed an anointing upon my life and has chosen me to serve a purpose for His glory. I therefore place my identity, my dignity and my future in His hands.

What I have experienced through government, bureaucracy and the various institutions and professionals involved in my circumstances has, in my view, been profoundly inconsistent with those qualities of God. I believe I have been abused, impoverished, disadvantaged, exiled and treated unjustly, and that falsehoods and institutional failures have contributed to that harm.

If God is the source of justice, love, truth and mercy, then injustice, cruelty, deliberate deception and the destruction of another person’s dignity cannot represent those qualities. I therefore reject the wrong that has been done to me without becoming consumed by hatred toward the individuals involved.

I claim no desire for revenge.

I have chosen forgiveness because I believe forgiveness is a grace that God has given me. I can forgive those who have participated in or contributed to what happened to me without pretending that it did not happen. Forgiveness does not require forgetting. It does not erase consequences, remove accountability, or require me to deny the truth of my experience.

Nor does forgiveness mean that I must accept continued injustice.

What grieves me most is not only the harm itself, but the apparent refusal to acknowledge it, apologise for it, or take responsibility where wrongdoing has been established. To me, treating forgiveness and grace with contempt is profoundly serious because those qualities are expressions of the God in whom I place my faith.

I therefore refuse to seek personal vengeance.

I leave judgment to God.

If there is a reckoning to come, let it come through truth, lawful accountability, conscience, circumstance and divine justice—not through anything I personally do to harm another person.

I have no wish to hurt anyone. I hold no malice toward those who have wronged me, because hatred does not serve me and revenge does not belong to me.

My warning is therefore not a threat.

It is a statement of conscience and faith:

Do not mistake forgiveness for consent.
Do not mistake mercy for weakness.
Do not mistake silence for forgetting.
Do not mistake the absence of personal revenge for the absence of accountability.

I believe that truth has consequences. I believe that injustice eventually demands an accounting. I believe that what is hidden can be brought into the light and that what has been falsely represented can ultimately be corrected.

I trust God with the outcome.

If I am vindicated, I will regard that vindication not as an opportunity for revenge, but as an opportunity for restoration, truth and justice.

I ask only that those who have played a part in my suffering recognise what happened, acknowledge the harm, and make amends where appropriate.

I have extended grace.

I have chosen forgiveness.

I have refused vengeance.

The rest I place in the hands of the Creator.
