Significance of the addressees

The list is a notice list, not a guilt list. A CC does not make an agency a co-conspirator. It creates a documentary question: who was told, on what date, and whether they recorded the report.

  • Police / integrity — NSW Police member mailboxes, Ombudsman NSW, IBAC, ICAC, NACC Inspector. A death-threat / family-conspiracy report was put on official addresses. A mailbox is not an investigation file until the agency says it opened one.
  • Disability / housing — Ablepoint, NDIS Commission / NDIA, TAG. Same week as the Ablepoint evidence request. If those bodies later say they were never told, the header is the contrary exhibit.
  • Legal aid and parliament — Legal Aid inbox, VOCAT, ministerial and parliamentary addresses. Significance for the Legal Aid brief: the author asked the system that exists to give people without private lawyers a path, while alleging that path is closed.
  • Human-rights / UN public desks — AHRC media, Amnesty, UN human-rights public addresses, ICC public affairs. International notice of a domestic complaint. An auto-reply is not a case number.
  • Media letters desks — Age, SMH, Guardian, ABC investigations and others. The claim was offered as a public-interest story. Silence from a letters desk is editorial, not a finding.
  • Named family and former intimates — addresses in the header. They were put on notice of the allegation about benefit from death. Being copied is not proof they planned a death. The test the author set in §11 is: produce or disprove the order — who signed it, who stood to benefit.

Significance of non-response

The author treats silence as targeting. This page does not. Non-response is a process fact.

What silence can mean: filtered mail; the mailbox is not a complaints channel; counsel advised no reply; the allegation was treated as already on file; the agency opened nothing. What silence does not mean: admission, participation, or that a murder was planned.

If no substantive reply is produced, the fair record is the sentence already in the Ablepoint letter: the concerns were put; no substantive response was provided. That is procedural history, not a verdict.

Public bodies have lawyers; the author sent the same text to dozens of inboxes without an equivalent representative. Documented refusal of Legal Aid is a resource fact. It is not proof of the family allegation.

Ablepoint letter — what it actually asks

  • §2 — preserve and identify agreements, notes, incidents, complaints, and allegations made about the author.
  • §4 — hypothetical “arrest me” lines are requests for lawful process, not admissions of rape, pedophilia, extortion or terrorism.

What would test the family / benefit claim

  • Any guardianship, family-violence, intervention or estate document the author says was signed.
  • Decision-maker, date, stated purpose.
  • Whether insurance, inheritance, property or a trust turned on incapacity or death.
  • Whether those persons now seek access to health or financial files, and on what authority.

Until those pages are produced, “conspiracy for my murder” stays an allegation in a subject line.
