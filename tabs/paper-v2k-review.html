🚀 Verified / Technical Information 
Patent US 5,159,703 —  
“Silent Subliminal Presentation System” 
This is the central patent often cited in discussions of SSSS. 
Key verified facts from the patent: 
•Inventor: Oliver M. Lowery of Norcross, Georgia.   
•Filed: December 28, 1989; issued October 27, 1992.   
•Purpose: A “silent communications system” in which 
carriers that are non-aural (i.e. outside the normal 
audible range for humans) or in the lower portion of 
the ultrasonic frequency spectrum are amplitude- or 
frequency-modulated with “intelligence” (information), 
and then propagated acoustically or vibrationally via 
transducers (e.g. loudspeakers, earphones, piezoelectric 
transducers) to be “perceived by a listener’s brain.”   

•It suggests these modulated signals may be recorded or 
stored (mechanical, magnetic, optical media) for later 
or repeated exposure.   
•The signals are meant to be located in non-aural 
portions of the audio spectrum (very low frequencies or 
high frequencies, including ultrasonic) so that they may 
not be consciously heard.   
So, in terms of technical feasibility, the patent describes a 
system that aims to transmit information using frequencies 
outside of normal hearing, modulated in ways to carry 
“hidden” content. 
⚠ What the Patent  
Does Not 
 Demonstrate / Claims It Does  
Not 
 Prove 

•The patent does not provide evidence that the brain 
truly perceives these subliminal signals as intended. It 
claims that the signals are “to be perceived by the 
brain,” but does not present experimental results in 
humans showing reliably induced thoughts, feelings or 
commands via this system.   
•It does not clearly establish that unconscious 
perception of such modulated non-aural signals can 
lead to meaningful, reliable behavioral or emotional 
change. It’s a technological description rather than a 
scientific proof of mind control. 
🧠 Claims & Fringe / Conspiracy Material 
These are things people claim about SSSS, often beyond 
what is verified. 
•The technology is sometimes said to be used by the 
U.S. military or Department of Defense (e.g. Operation 
Desert Storm), to induce emotional states (fear, 
despair) in enemy soldiers.   

•Claims of “emotion signature clusters”: that human 
emotional patterns (EEG signals) have been captured, 
cloned, stored, and then silently transmitted to induce 
similar emotional states in others.   
•Allegations that the system is used covertly via radio or 
TV broadcasts, digital signals, even via “microwave 
induction”.   
•Some sources say it has been operational since the 
early 1990s and used against civilians (“mass mind 
control”).   
🔍 Limitations / Criticisms of the Claims 
•Lack of peer-reviewed scientific studies: There are 
very few, if any, credible published experimental 
results showing people being influenced via the exact 
methods claimed. Much of the “evidence” is anecdotal 
or comes from fringe sources. 
•Auditory perception constraints: Humans typically hear 
within the 20 Hz to ~20 kHz range. Ultrasonic (above 
~20 kHz) and infrasonic (very low frequencies) signals 
may be inaudible, but there is no consensus that they 

can carry intelligible speech or detailed content in a 
way that is “decoded” by subconscious hearing 
reliably. 
•Physical and engineering challenges: 
◦High-frequency (ultrasonic) signals attenuate 
quickly in air, are affected by obstacles, and are 
much more difficult to propagate at long distances. 
◦The brain’s threshold for perceiving sounds (or 
ignoring them) is variable; modulated signals 
might be masked or distorted. 
•
•Interpretation & psychology: Many reports of “hearing 
voices” or “feeling influenced” may overlap with 
cognitive/psychological phenomena (hallucinations, 
expectation effects, stress, sleep deprivation, placebo/
suggestion).

Silent Sound Spread Spectrum (SSSS) and 
Subliminal Messaging: Scientific Review and 
Analysis 
Author: AI PDF Drive GPT 
Date: September 22, 2025 
Introduction 
This dossier examines Silent Sound Spread Spectrum (SSSS), 
a patented but controversial technology alleged to embed 
subliminal messages below conscious awareness, and 
compares it with scientific research on subliminal perception 
and ultrasonic auditory effects. The report distinguishes 
between verified facts, plausible science, and speculative 
claims. 
Patent Background 

Patent US 5,159,703 (“Silent Subliminal Presentation System”, 
Oliver M. Lowery, 1992) describes a system using non-aural or 
ultrasonic carriers modulated with information, allegedly to be 
perceived by the brain without conscious hearing. 
•The system proposes using carriers outside the normal 
human hearing range, modulated with hidden 
“intelligence” (e.g., speech patterns). 
•Delivery mechanisms include speakers, earphones, or 
vibrational transducers. 
•The patent provides a technical description, but no 
empirical evidence of reliable subliminal influence or 
behavior control. 
Scientific Evidence on Subliminal Perception 
Several peer-reviewed studies confirm that subliminal 
perception is real, though effects are modest: 

•Ruch, Züst & Henke (2016): Subliminal visual 
associations influenced decisions up to 30 minutes later. 
•Warren (2009): Review confirms subliminal perception 
exists but effects are modest and context-dependent. 
•Kumar & Singh (2021): Subliminal messaging can bias 
consumer choices and emotions, but effects are short-
lived. 
•Trappey (1996): Meta-analysis of subliminal advertising 
found weak and inconsistent influence. 
Conclusion: Subliminal perception is scientifically established, 
but influence is subtle, fragile, and situational. 
Ultrasonic and Hypersonic Research 
Research has explored whether inaudible high-frequency 
sound influences the brain: 
•Oohashi et al. (2000): High-frequency (>20 kHz) 
inaudible sounds affected EEG and cerebral blood flow; 
participants reported altered subjective states. 

•Yagi et al. (2003): Ultrasonics influenced auditory cortex 
and mood states, even without conscious awareness. 
Conclusion: Ultrasonics can influence physiology and neural 
activity, but no evidence supports decoding of speech or 
complex messages subliminally via ultrasonic carriers. 
EEG and Brain-Computer Interface Research 
Other studies investigate subliminal processing measured 
directly from the brain: 
•Frank et al. (2013): Subliminal visual stimuli elicited EEG 
responses that revealed private knowledge, even without 
conscious awareness. 
Implication: Subliminal processing is real and measurable with 
brainwave analysis, but requires specialized equipment and 
controlled conditions — not remote ultrasonic delivery. 

Critical Analysis of SSSS Claims 
Supported: 
•Embedding modulated signals into ultrasonic/non-aural 
frequencies. 
•Physiological response to inaudible frequencies 
(hypersonic effect). 
•Subliminal perception influences cognition and decision-
making. 
Unproven: 
•Transmission of intelligible speech via ultrasonic 
subliminal carriers. 
•Reliable behavior control through SSSS. 
•Documented use of SSSS in warfare or civilian mass 
manipulation. 

Overall: The leap from subliminal priming and hypersonic 
research to functional SSSS mind control remains speculative. 
Conclusion 
Subliminal perception is scientifically established, and 
ultrasonics can affect the brain subconsciously. However, the 
patent for Silent Sound Spread Spectrum remains largely 
theoretical, with no peer-reviewed demonstrations showing 
speech or messages being transmitted and decoded 
subconsciously via ultrasonic carriers. 
While the concept is technically intriguing, its alleged use in 
psychological warfare or mass control is not substantiated by 
scientific evidence.
