Timeline

Impartial chronology of dated records · not a verdict · Open the timeline page

Method. A date is included only if it is printed on a message in the Witness Remains PDF or if it is the date in a filename already listed on this archive’s index. An email date is the date of the message, not proof of the events described in it. A filename date is a catalogue date, not a verified event date. Allegations are labelled as allegations. Nothing below finds that a crime, conspiracy, or coordinated mandate occurred.

The earlier timeline on this site was a filter over filename-dated catalogue rows. That catalogue is still on the timeline page, underneath this narrative. It was not a chronology a reader could follow. This statement replaces that as the timeline tab. It does not delete the catalogue.

Read in order, the public index and this September 2026 email compilation show a long correspondence between one person and a series of agencies, then a concentrated burst of the author’s own emails in the first half of September 2026. The agency filenames show that letters and determinations exist and are dated. They do not, by themselves, show that each decision was unlawful, coordinated, or aimed at erasure. The September emails show what the author reported, to whom, and that he asked several recipients to preserve records and to separate his experience from a finding of fact. They do not show that the recipients agreed, investigated, or accepted the allegations.

Claims that reach further back — surveillance from at least 2002, a 2003 book, a 35-year span, fourteen psychiatric detentions, a clinical death in 2021, an assassination attempt — appear in the September emails as the author’s account. This timeline does not treat those as established events, because this compilation does not contain the primary record that would date them independently. They stay in the allegation column until a document on its face establishes them.

What a reader can actually do with this order

Start with the filename-dated agency letters, in the order above, and read those PDFs. Then read the September 2026 emails as a record of what was alleged afterwards and of how the author asked for the record to be preserved. Keep three columns: what an agency document states on its face, what the author reports experiencing, and what is still an unresolved question. This tab does not collapse those columns into one.
