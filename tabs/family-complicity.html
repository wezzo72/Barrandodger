13. ACTIVE PARTICIPATION VERSUS NON-INTERVENTION

These categories should not be collapsed.

  • A. Active participation — An identifiable action that directly contributed to an alleged event or outcome.
  • B. Knowing non-intervention — Evidence that a person knew of a situation and had some opportunity to intervene but did not do so.
  • C. Acceptance or endorsement — Evidence that a person accepted one account of events rather than another.

These are different propositions and require different evidence.
