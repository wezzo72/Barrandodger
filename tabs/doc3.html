From: Contact Centre (NDIS Commission) ContactCentre@ndiscommission.gov.au
Subject: RE: URGENT [SEC=OFFICIAL]
Date: 3 June 2021 at 10:54:37 am AEST
To: Rich McLean

Dear Rich

Thank you for contacting the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

NDIS providers are their own business, with usual business obligations. Being an NDIS provider does not take away the need for usual business insurances and practices. The audits you complete for us are for the purpose of your business to become a registered provider. This means your business has met a set of obligations and Practice standards the NDIS Commission holds to ensure that the services you provide NDIS participants are of a quality and safe nature. This process does not make you an employee of the NDIS Commission.

To confirm your employment status with the NDIA you would be best to direct your enquiry to them on enquiries@ndis.gov.au

Please don’t hesitate to contact us again should you have any further questions.

Yours sincerely
Sarah LeMaire
Senior Contact Centre Officer
