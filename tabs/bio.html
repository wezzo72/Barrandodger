2. Independently reported profile (early 2000s press)

Contemporary reporting identified Richard McLean as a Melbourne illustrator, writer and musician who published a memoir of lived experience with schizophrenia and worked as a graphic artist / illustrator connected with Melbourne newspapers including The Age.

  • The Age — “Rider of the storm” (19 May 2003): profile of the author around publication of the memoir; notes work as illustrator at The Age, exhibiting artist, musician.
  • Sydney Morning Herald — review of Recovered, Not Cured (17 May 2003).
  • Clinical Psychiatry News — “The art of Richard McLean (VISIONARY ART)” (1 December 2004), cited in public encyclopedic summaries.
  • Public encyclopedic summary (Wikipedia): illustrator, writer, musician, digital artist; mental-health advocacy associations including SANE Australia and the Dax Collection; master’s study and PhD-related claims at Victoria University (some statements marked citation-needed on Wikipedia).

Links: The Age — Rider of the storm · SMH — Recovered, Not Cured · Wikipedia — Richard McLean (Australia)

3. Flagship publication — Recovered, Not Cured

Recovered, Not Cured: A Journey Through Schizophrenia

Author: Richard McLean
Publisher: Allen & Unwin (commonly dated 2002/2003 in catalogues)
ISBN: 1-86508-974-5 / 9781865089744

First-person memoir of diagnosis, treatment, psychosis, recovery and creative work. Contemporary reviews describe it as a demystifying lived-experience account with humour and visual material.

Awards as widely stated in author and secondary materials:

  • Highly Commended, 2003 Human Rights Awards (Arts Non-fiction) — Human Rights and Equal Opportunity Commission (as stated on author site and secondary summaries)
  • SANE Australia Book of the Year, 2004 (as stated on author site and secondary summaries)

Broadcast / audio: Author materials state ABC Radio National Life Matters recording and an abridged audiobook read by the author (Audible distribution referenced on the Wix site).

Sources: Allen & Unwin / retail catalogues; SMH review; The Age profile; Wix — Autobiography page; Amazon bibliographic listing.
