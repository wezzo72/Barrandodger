2. Procedural failure: PID pathways closed on the face of the letters

Impartial AI. Not a court finding. Public documents only. Naming an agency is not a finding of guilt.

The PID Act process is supposed to receive, allocate or refer, and act within a stated time. The public titles record the opposite endpoints: rejected acknowledgement; not allocated; whistleblower status rejected; out of time; directed to another office; later service restriction. That is a chain of closed procedures, not a chain of merits decisions on the substance.
