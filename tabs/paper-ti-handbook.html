perpetrators to predict your movements and target you. This can include 
changing your routes to and from work, varying the times you leave your home, 
and altering your social activities. By being unpredictable, you reduce the 
likelihood of being targeted."

public. Be mindful of the information you share on social media and avoid 
discussing sensitive topics in public places. By keeping a low profile, you 
reduce the risk of being identified and targeted by perpetrators."

support."
