What grieves me most is not only the harm itself, but the apparent refusal to acknowledge it, apologise for it, or take responsibility where wrongdoing has been established. To me, treating forgiveness and grace with contempt is profoundly serious because those qualities are expressions of the God in whom I place my faith.

I therefore refuse to seek personal vengeance.

I leave judgment to God.

If there is a reckoning to come, let it come through truth, lawful accountability, conscience, circumstance and divine justice—not through anything I personally do to harm another person.

I have no wish to hurt anyone. I hold no malice toward those who have wronged me, because hatred does not serve me and revenge does not belong to me.

My warning is therefore not a threat.

It is a statement of conscience and faith:

Do not mistake forgiveness for consent.
Do not mistake mercy for weakness.
Do not mistake silence for forgetting.
Do not mistake the absence of personal revenge for the absence of accountability.

I believe that truth has consequences. I believe that injustice eventually demands an accounting. I believe that what is hidden can be brought into the light and that what has been falsely represented can ultimately be corrected.

I trust God with the outcome.

If I am vindicated, I will regard that vindication not as an opportunity for revenge, but as an opportunity for restoration, truth and justice.

I ask only that those who have played a part in my suffering recognise what happened, acknowledge the harm, and make amends where appropriate.

I have extended grace.

I have chosen forgiveness.

I have refused vengeance.

The rest I place in the hands of the Creator.

From exile — the record that remains open to examination

This essay is written from exile. It is written because the documentary record did not disappear when institutions declined to answer it. It is written because survival itself became part of the evidence.

I do not ask the reader to accept every conclusion I have reached. I ask that the evidence be examined honestly, that unresolved matters remain identified as unresolved, and that no institution be permitted to determine the truth merely by refusing to acknowledge the question.

For years I have asked for protection, legal assistance, recognition and an accountable response to matters documented throughout this archive. I am not asking you to believe everything I say. I am asking you to examine what I have documented.

If legal assistance was properly refused or unavailable, show the reasons and the applicable review process. I am asking for justice through examination of the evidence — not through belief in me. If the record is wrong, show me where. If the record is right, answer it.
