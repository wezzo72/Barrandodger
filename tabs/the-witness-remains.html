My situation is deliberate. It was not bureaucratic oversight, an error, an accident, or a misunderstanding.

People paid to care for me, such as Able Point, the NDIS, the NSW Trustee and Public Guardian, police, healthcare, the media, and politics in general, operate on a framework that, in my view, financially benefits from my continued harm while feigning care and ultimately aligning with my perpetrators. They deny my testimony, refuse to make meaningful changes to my circumstances, and then pretend that the harm they are paid to enact is nonexistent and that they have obeyed every duty of care without so much as a blemish on their perfect public facades.

Able Point claims that I can leave while being consciously aware that doing so is a financial and physical impossibility. Even if I could leave, there is no destination where I could go that would make me safer than behind the locked door of this entrapment scenario.

The culpable collective malice did not stop at my financial detriment. It was a coordinated groupthink whereby an entire society diluted the guilt of their hate by spreading my treatment thinly across every professional involved. The design was to break me and keep me unwell because, by keeping me below others, their positions were protected. They were aware that I was a threat to the illusions they had built their lives upon.

It was cruel.

The subtle detriment happened over years, with the goal of breaking my identity so that I would be confirmed as less than them. Their plan failed because my identity is not negotiable.

My silence was interpreted as weakness. My endurance meant agreement to them; they interpreted my continued survival and lack of immediate reaction as though the treatment was acceptable. But I am not quick to chaos, nor do I emotionally react or allow myself to be triggered into reacting to deliberate abuse that is intentionally causing me harm.

Eventually, I stopped showing up, choosing poverty but peace.

A spiritual debt was created that they cannot afford to pay. Intentionally hurting someone protected by the Most High God and anointed with favour for kingdom purposes is, in my understanding, a fight with the universe that no enemy of mine will win. It was spiritual sacrilege that both mocked and angered God and His plan for my life.

Left to people, society, or those paid to care for me, it became clear to me that I was not expected to survive. The intention, as I experienced it, was to erase me, erase my life, attack my person, and ultimately erase me even physically.

I do not have an allegiance with the government that gathered me. My allegiance is with the Creator under Christ.

God has protected me in circumstances in which I believe government would not protect anyone who stood alone with integrity and truth while being critical of it.

Any person who believed a comfortable lie about me while ignoring a difficult truth was, in my view, trading their integrity for the comfort, privileges, and relative safety of a government to which they had gifted their consent to be ruled by.

I could not be bribed or manipulated through the usual mechanisms of financial or political detriment, erosion of social standing or position, or threats to the very privileged jobs that claim to embody the personal ethical attributes required and mandated for the positions of privilege they hold.

I refused to be used as a tool for their agenda.

I experienced this as a cruel and calculated allegiance of professionals, agencies, places, the media, the legal fraternity, law enforcement, and everyone else, not shy of total and utter human abandonment, excommunication, and betrayal.

My presence disrupted their illusions.

Their panic turned into envy, then jealousy, and then malice.

They were comfortable mistreating me.
