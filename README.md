## 21 October 2026 · FORENSIC RESEARCH DOSSIER · not a finding

**FORENSIC RESEARCH DOSSIER — Patented Technologies, Human Perception, Subliminal Influence, Neuromodulation, Radio-Frequency Auditory Effects**

Primary-source patent analysis (163 pages) by Barran Dodger. Examines US patents from the 1950s–2000s on hypnosis apparatus, subliminal systems, sleep induction, electrotherapy, remote brain-wave proposals, silent subliminal presentation, and the radio-frequency hearing effect (including Air Force-assigned patents). Applies a five-level evidence framework. A patent is a disclosure, not proof of operational use against any person.
