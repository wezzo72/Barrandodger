And I should not have to watch my life reduced to a budget, a file number, an accommodation arrangement or a series of disconnected administrative decisions while the cumulative consequences remain inadequately addressed.

Public office is a responsibility to the public, not a sanctuary from accountability.

You may believe that your position provides protection from the consequences of decisions made within the system you serve. It does not remove your moral responsibility to consider what those decisions do to a human being. An official title does not make a person infallible. An administrative procedure does not automatically make an outcome just. Institutional loyalty cannot be permitted to replace independent judgment, compassion or the obligation to give reasons for decisions that affect another person’s life.
