Keep these labels apart

  • REFUGEE — legal status after an authorised determination
  • ASYLUM SEEKER — a claim made; not a grant
  • INTERNALLY DISPLACED PERSON — movement inside a country; not automatically a Convention refugee
  • COMPLEMENTARY PROTECTION / HUMAN RIGHTS CLAIM
  • DOCUMENTED DISPLACEMENT — a housing or removal record exists
  • CLAIMED DISPLACEMENT — author or advocate description
  • LEGAL CHARACTERISATION — possible reading of facts against a test
  • LEGAL DETERMINATION — none declared on this page
