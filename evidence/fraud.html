Fraud — concept, law and evidence analysis

“Fraud” is used here both as a legal concept requiring defined elements and as an investigative category for examining documentary inconsistencies. Inclusion in this index does not establish criminal liability.

No event is classified as criminal fraud on this page. A filename that contains the word fraud or corruption is AUTHOR’S ACCOUNT unless an official or judicial determination is cited.

Distinctions

  • fraud (legal finding) vs suspicion vs documentary inconsistency
  • deception / dishonesty / false representation / misleading conduct
  • civil fraud vs criminal fraud
  • administrative misconduct vs corruption as a legal characterisation
  • benefit, registration, corporate or identity-related issues — each needs its own elements

Elements before any later CLM- row may use the word fraud

  • conduct
  • representation
  • alleged false statement
  • evidence of falsity
  • knowledge
  • intent
  • reliance
  • loss or benefit
  • jurisdiction
  • primary documents
  • counter-evidence
  • official response / investigation / determination

If those fields cannot be filled from a primary file, status stays RESEARCH QUESTION or AUTHOR’S ALLEGATION.
