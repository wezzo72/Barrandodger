**Classification key used throughout**:  
- **Official record**: Document issued by a government agency, court, or statutory body (letterhead, decision, acknowledgment, or formal response).  
- **Allegation**: First-person statement, private record, or claim by the author or named individuals that has not been independently adjudicated.  
- **Inference**: Analytical conclusion drawn from the juxtaposition of official records and allegations; requires independent examination.

## Methodological Statement

This finding aid preserves the original documents exactly as they appear in the public GitHub repositories. No original file has been altered. Interpretation, classification (official record / allegation / inference), and the selection of items are the work of the compiler and are clearly labelled as such. Readers are directed to open the linked source files and form their own assessment.

The strongest currently documented cluster for potential administrative action concerns NDIS provider obligations (particularly alleged failures to report reportable incidents under the NDIS Act 2013 and associated Code of Conduct / quality and safeguards requirements) involving Able Point / AbleCare and related support arrangements. This is evidenced by the 14 October 2026 private record, the April 2026 call transcript, NDIS plan and compilation documents, and the broader government ZIP archive.
