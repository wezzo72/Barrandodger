
THE MIRROR

God’s Favour, Anointing, and Purpose — The Calling of a Chosen Vessel for His Glory

The government’s failed plans of the past have no power over God’s plan or the purpose He has ordained for my life.

By Barran Dodger 


https://barrandodgerarchive.base44.app

Some stories begin with a victory; mine begins with survival. THE MIRROR is an account of what I believe happened when a person refused to surrender his testimony, his conscience, or his faith—and what happened when the institutions he trusted appeared, in his experience, to become the very forces he was forced to confront. This is not simply a story about government, bureaucracy, suffering, or survival. It is a story about what remains when everything that once gave a person security is stripped away: the evidence, the conscience, the witness, and God. I invite the reader to look beyond the accusations, beyond the labels, and beyond the public version of events, and to examine the record for themselves. What you will encounter is my testimony of survival, my preserved archive, and my belief that the plans of human institutions—however powerful they may once have appeared—cannot ultimately determine the purpose God has ordained for a life. They may have tried to erase the witness; they did not erase the purpose.

Mobbing is the safety of the crowd: believing a comfortable lie over a hard truth. The same government to which people give their allegiance is, in my experience and according to the evidence I have documented, the same system I say has been responsible for targeting me through administrative annihilation, abuse, neglect, surveillance, targeting, violence, psychiatric weaponisation, political exile, alleged assassination attempts, institutional harm, family violence, financial abuse, and the blocking or denial of pathways to legal aid and justice.

To admit that you participated in such treatment would mean confronting a breach of the very standards of human decency, professional conduct, legal responsibility, ethics and morality that privileged positions claim to uphold. It would mean admitting to having ganged up on a vulnerable person. That is a cowardly position that few people can accept or admit to because, in my view, it represents the height of moral cowardice. It is a low act.

Everyone relies on government to protect and save them from the very outrage that government can itself create, producing an endless cycle in which outrage is created and then someone plays the saviour, amplifying their political significance or importance.

No one has opposed my exile. No one has meaningfully opposed my early death, from which I was revived. My evidence is fact-checked, evidence-based and published. Yet the reason I believe people do not acknowledge it is because character assassination, libel and slander have created a false impression of me as someone of no worth.

Where are the facts behind the allegations made against me?

There are none.

Because the devil is a liar and a coward.

Not one person has looked me in the eye with the courage of their convictions and presented evidence capable of proving the allegations against me. It is the oldest trick in the book: slander the person speaking the truth when that person is standing alone because they refuse to sell out to the false illusion upon which everyone else has based their lives.

Your jobs depend on it. Your income depends on it. Your comfort depends on it. Your social standing and societal privilege depend on it.

I am banned from Legal Aid, the Ombudsman and the anti-corruption commission. I cannot report a crime to police or access a meaningful pathway to justice.

The general public prefers the safety of the crowd: blending in, accepting the comfort of the mob and believing that conformity provides protection. To me, that mob appears to include professionals across society—police, lawyers, politicians, public officials, journalists—and even former family and friends.

I only have God and my dog.

I would rather have only God and stand alone than join the mob and trade my integrity, intelligence and commitment to truth for the lie that government will protect you. The evidence I have experienced tells me something different: that the same system people trust to protect them can also erase the person who challenges it.

I would rather stand alone with the courage of my convictions, with my intelligence, ethics and morals intact, unable to be bribed, guilted, manipulated, corrupted or paid off. I refuse to become dependent upon the same bureaucracy that people simultaneously complain about while continuing to grant it permission to rule and control their lives.

People complain about corrupt politicians, banks, big business and the powers that be. They complain about government wrongdoing while simultaneously surrendering their ability to critique the bureaucracy they serve. They accept limited privileges from the system while remaining outraged at its corruption. Yet when that same bureaucracy is accused of causing profound harm to one person, the criticism suddenly becomes too uncomfortable to acknowledge.

Instead of accepting criticism as an opportunity for government to examine itself and improve, I believe the system chose to erase, exile and sabotage the person who refused to accept the lie or participate in the corruption.

If I die as a result of abuse, neglect, poverty, surveillance, electronic harassment, exile, isolation or entrapment—conditions that I say have already contributed to suicidal crisis—then people can later pretend they did not know. They can blame me for being mentally ill, unstable or too far gone.

But to cause detriment to a vulnerable person and then blame that person for becoming angry, unstable or distressed when they raise their voice has a name: emotional abuse. When that conduct is collectively sustained by a societal mob, it represents, in my view, the height of moral cowardice.

Not a single person reading this will want to admit that they participated in it.

Because it is humiliating to admit.

It requires someone to admit that they abandoned the integrity, fact-checking, intelligence, humanity, morality and ethical fortitude that they claim to possess.

I see it differently.

I have come to believe that people are capable of evil while simultaneously convincing themselves that they are good. They think I am too pure to see them. They think I will be tricked. They believe they can preserve their perfect public facade while participating in or remaining silent about the targeting and sacrifice of a vulnerable, gay, disabled and unprotected whistleblower who refused to surrender his convictions and who insists that he has evidence to support what he says.

Everyone pretends they do not see it. They pretend they do not see me, my case or my circumstances. They say they did not know what was happening, or that they still do not know what is happening. And if they deceived both me and themselves, they can continue believing they are good people, worthy of their social standing and privileges, while denying me the same qualities they accept in themselves.

I know the devil. I have been to hell and shaken his hand. I rearranged the furniture and came back with the blueprints of hell.

I understand darkness.

I am both dark and light.

I align myself with the Creator, under Christ and the Most High God.

I use my knowledge of darkness to understand the human condition: the streak of evil that human beings can embody while convincing themselves that they do not embody it. The everyday person can tell themselves they are good while simultaneously participating in, tolerating or ignoring something profoundly wrong.

Society convinced itself that I was disposable.

That I could be erased.

Systemically, politically and financially destroyed, attacked and desecrated—not merely because I spoke the truth, but because my existence became a challenge to the illusion that everyone else had built their comfort and privilege upon.

I understand this as spiritual warfare as well: an attack not only upon my person and identity, but upon me spiritually, through an allegiance in which a mob, acting through cowardice, ganged up on one person.

My truth threatened the very illusions upon which that mob had built its concepts of comfort and privilege. People lived believing a lie that they were either too blinded to see clearly, unwilling to confront, or afraid to acknowledge. Some may have known the lie and known the evil, yet convinced themselves they were still good people. Others may have known something was wrong and simply refused to correct it at the expense of the one person telling the truth.

I stood up to a society that, in my view, offered me as a human sacrifice and scapegoat because facing its own evil—its own lie—was too much truth to bear.

I never meant those people harm.

All I did was hold up a mirror.

And you could not stand to see the ugliness of your own reflections.

So you attacked the reflection of yourselves in the mirror I held up to you.

You were not even attacking me.

This was never personal.

You attacked the reflection of yourselves that you could not bear to look at.

Because facing the truth about what you did, what you permitted, or what you are still doing is unbearable for anyone determined to maintain the belief that they are a good person.

It is easier to dump the collective sins, failures, cowardice and wrongdoing of an entire alliance, society or group onto one person and sacrifice that person than to acknowledge the truth about yourselves.

I became the scapegoat because the mirror was easier to destroy than the reflection.

GOD HAS MY BACK

But God has my back.

When the government played God with my life and treated me as a sacrifice, it crossed a boundary far greater than political power, institutional authority or bureaucratic control. That audacity angered the God who anointed me, the God who preserved my life and allowed me to survive so that I could expose the truth contained within my archive.

I believe I was preserved for a purpose: to document what happened, to preserve the evidence, to expose corruption and evil, and to become a witness to a reckoning that ultimately belongs to God.

I understand that reckoning as a form of universal balance—a spiritual physics underpinning existence itself, a fundamental law of nature that cannot ultimately be cancelled, corrupted or overruled by human bureaucracy.

God is eternal, perfect and loving. God loves those who honour Him and wants people to be forgiven so that they can be free and at peace.

Government, by comparison, is fleeting and imperfect. Institutions rise and fall. Governments change. Bureaucracies eventually disappear. Yet institutions can become cruel when they perceive something as a threat to their existence and can attempt to destroy whatever challenges their authority.

Now think about God.

What existential threat could possibly frighten the eternal Creator?

God is everything across the totality of time, existence and creation. Nothing human beings build can ultimately rival that.

That is why I no longer live under the psychological allegiance that I once believed society demanded of me.

I have broken free from allegiance to a bureaucracy to which almost everyone has given some form of consent and permission to rule and control their lives.

I do not need the government.

I survived without it.

More than that, I survived what I believe were malicious attempts to erase and destroy me.

I am still alive.

I believe I am anointed with the favour and protection of a God who has my back.

I have no allegiance to the government I say targeted me through a collective sacrifice of the mob.

I am an independent and sovereign spiritual witness.

They failed to erase me.

They failed to destroy me.

I am alive.

For me, that is evidence of God.

My written testimony and my preserved evidence are the foundation of my truth. I believe I was allowed to document and preserve that testimony by the Most High God, without whom I believe I would be dead—and, in a profound sense, I was already brought back from death.

I survived a fatal injury and was revived by circumstances I understand as an act of God’s preservation. I then experienced what I understood as almost total human betrayal, leaving me with one ultimate source of reliance: God.

That experience did not destroy my faith.

It fortified it.

It revealed a purpose for my future.

I no longer want my life to be about revenge, recognition, status or retaliation.

I want my life to be a vessel for God’s glory and for His purposes.

That is extraordinary to me.

I do not mean harm to any person.

I do not need revenge.

I hold no hatred.

I do not want to destroy the people who I believe tried to destroy me.

But I believe those who participated in attempts to erase or sacrifice me are frightened by the existence of my testimony, because the testimony remains.

The archive remains.

The evidence remains.

And I remain.

What concerns me is that, even after everything that has happened, I believe some of those involved continue to embody malice toward me while simultaneously pretending they are not participating in the harm or detriment.

I will not respond to that with hatred.

I will not become what I believe was done to me.

I will not surrender my integrity.

I will not surrender my faith.

I will not surrender the truth as I understand it.

I leave vengeance where Scripture says it belongs.

Revenge is God’s alone.

My responsibility is to remain alive, preserve the evidence, tell the truth as faithfully as I can, forgive where I am able, and use whatever life God has preserved for me for a purpose greater than myself.

They attempted to erase the witness.

The witness remains.

They attempted to destroy the testimony.

The testimony remains.

They attempted to make me disappear.

I am still here.

And I believe that is not the end of the story.

It is the beginning of my purpose.

Barran Dodger
