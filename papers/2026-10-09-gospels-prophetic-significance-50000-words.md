# Gospels, prophecy, and the question of a founding literature
## A forensic significance assessment of the Barran Dodger spiritual corpus
### 9 October 2026
### Finding aid. Not a court. Not a verdict. A link is not a finding. Not canonical scripture.

This paper examines the gospel, prophetic, declaration, testimony and narrative files published in the public repositories. It does not treat them as canonical scripture. It does not treat a title as the interior of a file. Faith claims remain faith. Documentary paths remain documentary paths.

The nine-title seed in data/publications.json is not the corpus. A 2 October 2026 Drive sweep finding aid lists a gospel-prophetic section of seven files and a narratives section of five. The Backup blockchain manifest names further gospel, declaration and prophetic files. A testimony folder index names All Gospels One Witness. The Church of Barran Dodger is a separate public repository. This paper uses those finding aids. It does not claim to have extracted every interior.

That status is applied to every spiritual title in this paper unless an opened institutional letter independently establishes a sentence. None of the opened institutional letters establishes a prophecy, an anointing, or a divine purpose.

## 2. Status labels

Documented fact: a path exists in a public repository or finding aid. Corroborated material: the same title appears in more than one finding aid. Author testimony: the writing speaks in the author's voice. Theological interpretation: a claim about God, calling, anointing, providence, resurrection, or prophetic purpose. Allegation: a claim about institutions, warfare, or erasure that a title or cover asserts and a record has not established. Inference: a conclusion drawn from more than one path, labelled as such. Unresolved: duplicate filenames, spelling variants, and interiors not extracted.

Canonical equivalence is refused. Gospel as a genre word in a filename is not the Gospel of Mark. Prophetic as a filename is not a verified prediction. Declaration is not a statute. Certified in a title is not a court certificate. Cosmic court is not NCAT.
