Scope

This is a documentary finding aid. It is not a court, a tribunal, an official investigation, or a determination of liability. Inclusion of a document does not establish wrongdoing, motive, unlawful conduct, or personal responsibility.

The review looked at the public repository wezzo72/Barrandodger at commit 1b98e6efea73f2633ac089cba0594cf968fd7cac on 11 October 2026. The tree contained 6,617 files, including 3,647 PDFs. This register does not claim to list all of them.

What was covered. Files in docs/official-drive/, the text-extracted copies used to read them, and selected files in imports/official-2026-10-01/ whose text was opened. Where two files had the same SHA-256, they are one entry with the other path listed as a copy.

What was not covered. The site mirror, the barrandodger.com website backup, rendered HTML, AI essays, gospels, prophecy, YouTube transcripts, and the great majority of the 3,647 PDFs were not opened and are not treated as government findings. Eleven larger files inside docs/official-drive/ were located and listed, but their text was not extracted. Several PDFs returned no text layer.

Last review: 11 October 2026. Preservation first. Verification second. Context third. Conclusions last.

Genesis of this page

This register was constructed under the constraints of the Master Build Command for the Barran Dodger Archive. The command required an impartial finding aid, strict separation of documentary status, and no conversion of allegation into fact. Readers can inspect the full instruction set that governed the construction of the presentation layer.

Audit of the 3,647 PDFs

The tree at the audit commit contained 3,647 PDFs. Opening pages of 115 PDFs were extracted and hashed for this register. After identical copies were collapsed, the register holds the entries shown above. The remaining PDFs were not text-inspected in this pass and are not treated as government findings solely by their presence in the repository.

All files remain available for independent examination:

  • Full repository tree at the audit commit
  • docs/official-drive (official binaries)
  • wezzo72/Backup documents (government-evidence and related)
  • Existing official government PDFs catalogue
  • Expanded official catalogue

A filename is not a finding. Further systematic extraction of the remaining PDFs can be added to this register without removing any existing entry. This section records the scope of the current review and points to the complete public store.
